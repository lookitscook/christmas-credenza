Drag to explore emotions, with up to 20 points across the visible surface. Only the four nearest the reticle have labels, and only during dragging. The dropdown shows the selected emotion when at rest. Hover over any point to inspect it, or click a point or label to select it. Drag the ring clockwise from low on the left to high on the right to change intensity; the bottom gap is inactive. Release to ease to the nearest emotion. The surface, reticle, and YUV swatch show the current intensity’s color; each landmark dot shows that emotion’s color. Double-click to return to neutral.

Keyboard: arrow keys rotate · + / − change intensity · Home returns to neutral.
